## Logo

The bean mark and the FreshBrew lockup are original work made for this project,
not licensed assets.

The mark is a single filled path with an evenodd crease cut through it, which
means no `<mask>` and no element id that could collide when the logo is inlined
more than once on a page. It is drawn in `src/components/brand/Logo.tsx` and
stays legible down to 16px. `src/app/icon.svg`, `apple-icon.png` and
`favicon.ico` are all generated from the same path.

The wordmark sets "Fresh" roman and "Brew" italic in the accent colour — the
same roman-then-italic move the section headings use — so the logo reads as
part of the site's voice rather than a badge applied to it.

## Video

`public/videos/website_video1.mp4` was supplied by the client. It is 10.68s,
970x720, 7.4MB, and drives the whole homepage scroll story.

Two things to fix before launch:

- **Resolution.** 970px wide upscales on any display past about 1000px, which
  softens the footage. A 1920x1080 master would render cleanly.
- **Keyframe spacing.** Scroll scrubbing seeks constantly, and each seek decodes
  from the nearest keyframe. Re-encoding with a keyframe every few frames makes
  scrubbing noticeably smoother, at some cost in file size:

  ```bash
  ffmpeg -i website_video1.mp4 -an -vf scale=1920:-2 \
    -c:v libx264 -crf 24 -g 6 -keyint_min 6 -sc_threshold 0 \
    -movflags +faststart website_video1.mp4
  ```

  Dropping the audio track (`-an`) is safe: the video is always muted.

`public/videos/website_video2.mp4` and `website_video3.mp4` are unused.
